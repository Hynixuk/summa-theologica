Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Summa Theologica reading app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it's handled. The app is a free, non-commercial reading and study tool for Aristotle's Metaphysics, Aquinas's Summa Contra Gentiles and Summa Theologica, and Augustine's On the Trinity — public-domain texts with audio narration, search, quizzes, and optional account sync.

1. Who is responsible for your data

This app is an independent project maintained by Petra Anwanina. For any privacy question or request, contact:

Email: petraanwanina@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Account data (only if you sign in): your email address, used solely to send a passwordless sign-in link and to identify your account. We never see or store a password.
  • Reading progress (only if you sign in): which chapters/articles you've marked read, your quiz scores, and your last reading/audio position — so this can follow you across devices.
  • Reading progress (if you don't sign in): the same information, but kept only in your browser's local storage on that one device — never sent to us.
  • Technical data: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) generated automatically by our hosting provider, Vercel, for security and reliability. We do not run separate analytics or advertising trackers.

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or payment information — the app has no purchases, subscriptions, or billing of any kind.

3. On-device AI search — nothing leaves your browser

The app's optional "AI" search toggle runs a small language model entirely inside your own browser (via WebGPU), not on any server we or a third party operate. Your questions and the app's answers are never transmitted anywhere — the model itself is a one-time download (cached by your browser) from a public CDN, not a data channel back to us. If your device can't run it, the app falls back to ordinary on-page keyword search, which is likewise entirely local.

4. Why we process this data

  • To provide core features: displaying text and audio, keyword search, and quizzes.
  • To let you sign in and sync your reading progress across your own devices, if you choose to create an account.
  • To keep the app secure and running reliably (basic hosting-provider logs).

5. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Consent — creating an account and signing in is entirely optional and opt-in; you can use the full app without ever providing an email address.
  • Legitimate interests — for basic security/reliability logs generated by our hosting provider.

6. Who we share data with

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising. A small number of infrastructure providers process data on our behalf, strictly to run the app:

We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. International data transfers

Our providers (Supabase, Vercel) may process data outside your home country, including in the United States. Where this happens, it relies on the safeguards those providers themselves maintain (such as standard contractual clauses).

8. How long we keep data

Account and synced progress data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and its associated data at any time by emailing us. Locally-stored (never-signed-in) progress lives only in your own browser and is deleted whenever you clear your browser's site data.

9. Security

Synced data is protected by database-level access rules that restrict it to your own signed-in account only (nobody else, including us in normal operation, can read another user's progress through the app). Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we don't collect more than the app needs to function.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent (e.g. by signing out and deleting your account) at any time. To exercise any of these, email petraanwanina@gmail.com — we'll respond within a reasonable time, and in any event within one month.

If you're in the UK, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. If you're in the EEA, you may contact your local data protection authority.

11. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

12. Cookies and local storage

The app doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember things like your reading position, theme preference, and (if you're signed out) your progress — this data stays on your device and is never sent to us unless you sign in, at which point it's synced only to let you access it from your other devices.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
